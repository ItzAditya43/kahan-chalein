    How it's counted

    Honest notes

    • People are GoatCounter's unique visitors: one person on one device in a session. Reloading doesn't add one.
    • Searches count only when someone picks their own city, time or mood. The picks shown when the page opens are not counted.
    • Shares are taps on "Send to the group chat", Share, Copy link or Story image. Whether the message was then sent is not known.
    • Friends brought in are people who opened a link someone sent them: a single trip, or a group plan.
    • Some link-preview bots run JavaScript and get counted, so the first few visits on launch day are probably bots.
    • The numbers on this page refresh every few minutes. The full raw data is public on GoatCounter.
