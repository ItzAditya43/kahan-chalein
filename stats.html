    How it's counted
    • People are GoatCounter's unique visitors: one person on one device in a session. Reloading doesn't add one.
    • Searches count only when someone picks their own city, time or mood, not the picks shown when the page opens.
    • Shares are taps on "Send to the group chat", Share, Copy link, Story image, Result image, "Send these to the group" (best bets) or "Send the split". Whether the message was then sent is not known.
    • Friends brought in are people who opened a link someone sent them: a single trip, a group plan, or this weekend's best bets.
    • Some link-preview bots run JavaScript and get counted, so the first few visits on launch day are probably bots.
    • Refreshes every few minutes. Full raw data is public on GoatCounter.
